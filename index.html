📣 Marketing – Tell people about a business.

What is social media?

Social media is a way people connect, communicate, and share information online.

People share hobbies, pictures, news, ideas, and business information.

What is a caption?

A caption is a few words that describe or explain a picture.

🐕🛁

Example caption: "Buddy enjoys a fresh bath!"

Try writing a caption for a picture of a cat playing.

📣 Social Media and Business

Businesses use social media to tell people about their products and services.

What is an advertisement?

An advertisement is a message that encourages people to learn about or buy something.

What is a headline?

A headline is a title that gets people's attention.

What is a caption?

A caption describes a picture.

Practice: Which one is an advertisement?

Choose the best answer.
